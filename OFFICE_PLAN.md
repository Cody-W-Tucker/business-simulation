# Knowledge-office staffing sketch

This is a fictional, single-shift layout scenario for a consulting, marketing,
and development business, not an industry benchmark or financial forecast.
It uses **26 employees** as a visual planning point near $5 million annual revenue.
The same headcount is not assumed viable across the whole $2–10 million range.

| Area | People | Roles represented |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Sales / client services bullpen | 6 | Sales 4, client services 2 |
| Shared and private offices | 12 | CFO 1, marketing 1, sales and client services managers 2, product manager 1, IT / engineering / operations 4, HR and legal 2, COO 1 |
| CEO office | 1 | CEO 1 |
| Specialist corridor | 6 | Website developer 1, content and design 1, consultant 1, service delivery manager 1, knowledge specialist 1, project manager 1 |
| Reception | 1 | Receptionist 1 |
| Projects / research | 0 | Shared whiteboard space, not assigned staff |
| **Total** | **26** | All counts are people, not additional seats or shifts |

For the explorable layout and placeholder interaction limits, see
[README.md](README.md#world-scale-and-building-on-the-project).
At this scale some specialist functions could instead be part-time, shared, or
outsourced; the distinct departments are a visual organization, not a hiring rule.

## Productivity and affordability

Revenue per employee is annual revenue divided by employee count. For 26 people,
it is approximately $77,000 at $2 million, $192,000 at $5 million, and $385,000
at $10 million. These are arithmetic scenarios, not forecasts. In particular,
$2 million may be difficult for 26 people once pay and overhead are included.
Check actual billable utilization, rates, labor costs, contractor costs, and
other expenses before treating any staffing level as financially workable.

Profit per employee uses net profit rather than revenue. Earnings per share is
net earnings attributable to common shareholders divided by weighted-average
shares outstanding; it is not a staffing metric and cannot be inferred from
revenue or headcount alone. The [Census Annual Business Survey](https://www.census.gov/data/tables/2022/econ/abs/2022-abs-company-summary.html)
provides firm data by industry and size, but no industry-specific average was
selected for this generic scenario.

## Deferred expansion

No production wing, warehouse, raw-goods flow, or loading area is planned for
this knowledge-based business. A future expansion can be scoped around actual
consulting and development workflows once the required rooms and capacity are
known. The research hub's north wall remains intact until that plan is decided.
