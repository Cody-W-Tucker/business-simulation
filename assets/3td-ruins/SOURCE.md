# 3TD Fantasy Ruins Pack

`ruined_arch.glb` is the RuinArchOne200 mesh from the 3TD Fantasy Ruins Pack
by Ron Kapaun / 3TD Studios, CC0: https://opengameart.org/content/3td-fantasy-ruins-pack

The textured Blender conversion was prepared by velocirection:
https://opengameart.org/content/3td-fantasy-ruins-pack-for-blender
The arch was exported separately to glTF with its origin moved to (0, 0, 0).
This trial has no collision; existing courtyard and board collisions are unchanged.
