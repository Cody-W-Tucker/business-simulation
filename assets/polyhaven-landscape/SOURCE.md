All files below are by Poly Haven contributors and distributed under CC0 1.0:

Downloaded 2026-11-01; original files are unmodified.

- `meadow_2_4k.hdr`: Meadow 2, by Sergej Majboroda,
  https://polyhaven.com/a/meadow_2 (4K HDR panorama; currently used).
- `stone_floor_diff_4k.jpg` and `stone_floor_nor_gl_4k.jpg`: Stone Floor,
  by Dimitrios Savva, https://polyhaven.com/a/stone_floor (4K color and OpenGL
  normal, currently used on the courtyard).
- `white_sandstone_blocks_02_diff_1k.jpg` and `white_sandstone_blocks_02_nor_gl_1k.jpg`:
  White Sandstone Blocks 02, by Rob Tuytel,
  https://polyhaven.com/a/white_sandstone_blocks_02 (1K color and OpenGL normal).
Poly Haven does not provide a version number for these downloads; the filenames,
resolution, and download date identify the selected releases. The active files
are pinned in `assets/downloads.nix`.
